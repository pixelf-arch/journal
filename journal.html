Journal

I made this for personal use. A quiet, beautiful space for writing and to-dos.
Everything stays on your computer. Nothing is ever uploaded or shared.

Step 1
Install the app
Click the install icon (⊕) in Chrome's address bar
Step 2
Choose a folder
Pick where your entries will live on your Mac
Step 3
Open at login
Right-click the app in your Dock → Options → Open at Login
